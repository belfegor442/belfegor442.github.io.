1. PURPOSE
   ============================================================

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") establishes the rules governing
the acceptable use of Weird Stuff products, applications, websites,
services, infrastructure, communication channels, and other
resources covered by this Policy.

The purpose of this Policy is to protect Users, Weird Stuff,
third parties, infrastructure, intellectual property, and the
availability and security of Weird Stuff services.

============================================================
2. SCOPE
========

This Policy applies to Users and other persons using a Weird Stuff
service where the service refers to this Policy.

It may apply to:

* Applications.
* Software.
* Websites.
* Digital services.
* Accounts.
* APIs.
* Custom applications.
* Communication systems.
* Community spaces operated by Weird Stuff.
* Other resources explicitly designated by Weird Stuff.

[PENDING]

The exact services covered by this Policy must be identified
before publication.

============================================================
3. GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
=======================

[MANDATORY]

Users must:

* Use Weird Stuff services lawfully.
* Follow the applicable Terms and Conditions.
* Respect the rights of other Users and third parties.
* Respect security controls.
* Use resources only for their intended purposes.
* Provide accurate information where information is required.
* Follow product-specific rules.

[MANDATORY]

Users must not deliberately misuse Weird Stuff systems,
resources, accounts, or services.

============================================================
4. UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS
======================

[MANDATORY]

Users must not:

* Access another person's account without authorization.
* Attempt to obtain credentials belonging to another person.
* Circumvent authentication.
* Bypass access controls.
* Exploit security restrictions for unauthorized access.
* Access systems or information without permission.
* Maintain unauthorized access after discovering it.

[MANDATORY]

Possessing technical knowledge or discovering a vulnerability does
not itself create authorization to access restricted systems.

============================================================
5. SECURITY TESTING AND VULNERABILITIES
=======================================

Security research must remain within the scope of any applicable
authorization.

[MANDATORY]

Users must not knowingly exploit a vulnerability to:

* Obtain unauthorized data.
* Obtain credentials.
* Damage systems.
* Interrupt services.
* Escalate privileges without authorization.
* Establish persistent unauthorized access.
* Harm another User.

[RECOMMENDED]

Security vulnerabilities should be reported through the official
security reporting channel instead of being publicly disclosed
before Weird Stuff has had a reasonable opportunity to respond.

============================================================
6. MALWARE AND MALICIOUS CODE
=============================

[MANDATORY]

Users must not intentionally use Weird Stuff services to:

* Distribute malware.
* Deploy ransomware.
* Distribute credential-stealing software.
* Operate malicious payloads.
* Distribute destructive code.
* Create or distribute software intended to compromise systems.
* Conceal malicious activity through Weird Stuff infrastructure.

This restriction applies regardless of whether the target is
Weird Stuff or a third party.

============================================================
7. FRAUD AND DECEPTION
======================

[MANDATORY]

Users must not use Weird Stuff services for:

* Fraud.
* Credential theft.
* Phishing.
* Deliberate impersonation.
* Identity deception.
* Financial scams.
* Social engineering intended to obtain unauthorized access,
  credentials, money, or sensitive information.

[MANDATORY]

Users must not falsely represent themselves as an official
representative of Weird Stuff.

============================================================
8. ABUSE OF INFRASTRUCTURE
==========================

[MANDATORY]

Users must not intentionally:

* Overload services.
* Consume unreasonable resources through abuse.
* Deliberately cause denial of service.
* Interfere with infrastructure.
* Circumvent resource limitations.
* Use automated processes designed to impair service operation.
* Deliberately generate excessive malicious traffic.

[PENDING]

Specific technical resource limits may be established for
individual services.

============================================================
9. AUTOMATION AND SCRAPING
==========================

Automation may be permitted where a service supports it.

[MANDATORY]

Users must not use automation to:

* Circumvent technical restrictions.
* Evade security controls.
* Flood services.
* Harvest restricted information.
* Circumvent rate limits.
* Interfere with normal operation.
* Obtain information that the User is not authorized to access.

[PENDING]

Where applicable, individual services may define API and
automation-specific requirements.

============================================================
10. PRIVACY AND PERSONAL DATA
=============================

[MANDATORY]

Users must not intentionally use Weird Stuff services to collect,
expose, sell, distribute, or otherwise misuse another person's
personal data without appropriate authorization or legal basis.

[MANDATORY]

Users must not publicly expose sensitive information obtained
through Weird Stuff services for malicious or unauthorized
purposes.

[MANDATORY]

Users must respect the Privacy Policy and applicable data
protection requirements.

============================================================
11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
=========================

[MANDATORY]

Users must not knowingly use Weird Stuff services to infringe the
intellectual property rights of others.

This includes unauthorized use of:

* Copyrighted material.
* Trademarks.
* Software.
* Designs.
* Digital assets.
* Documentation.
* Other protected material.

[MANDATORY]

Users must comply with applicable licenses when using third-party
software or content.

============================================================
12. IMPERSONATION AND BRAND ABUSE
=================================

[MANDATORY]

Users must not:

* Impersonate Weird Stuff.
* Pretend to be an authorized representative.
* Create deceptive accounts intended to appear official.
* Falsely claim a partnership with Weird Stuff.
* Misuse Weird Stuff branding to deceive Users.

[MANDATORY]

Official communications must only be issued through authorized
channels or by authorized representatives.

============================================================
13. HARASSMENT AND ABUSIVE CONDUCT
==================================

[MANDATORY]

Users must not intentionally use Weird Stuff services to engage in
serious harassment, threats, intimidation, or targeted abuse.

[MANDATORY]

Threats involving physical harm, credible criminal activity, or
serious safety risks may be escalated through appropriate channels
where permitted or required.

============================================================
14. ILLEGAL USE
===============

[MANDATORY]

Weird Stuff services must not knowingly be used for activities
that violate applicable law.

Users remain responsible for ensuring that their use of Weird Stuff
services is lawful in their relevant circumstances.

============================================================
15. CUSTOM APPLICATION REQUESTS
===============================

Custom application requests are subject to additional review.

[MANDATORY]

Users must not request that Weird Stuff knowingly create or
provide software intended to facilitate:

* Unauthorized access.
* Malware distribution.
* Credential theft.
* Fraud.
* Destructive attacks.
* Illegal surveillance.
* Abuse of third-party systems.
* Other prohibited activity.

[MANDATORY]

Weird Stuff may reject requests that create unacceptable legal,
security, operational, or technical risks.

Additional requirements may be defined in the Custom Applications
Policy.

============================================================
16. CONTENT
===========

Where Weird Stuff services permit Users to upload or submit
content:

[MANDATORY]

Users must not intentionally submit content for purposes that
violate applicable law or the applicable service rules.

[MANDATORY]

Users must not knowingly submit malware or malicious executable
content where such content is prohibited by the service.

[MANDATORY]

Users must not use submission systems to attack, test, or disrupt
the service without authorization.

============================================================
17. SECURITY CONTROLS
=====================

[MANDATORY]

Users must not intentionally circumvent or disable security
controls implemented to protect Weird Stuff services, accounts,
data, or infrastructure.

This includes, where applicable:

* Authentication mechanisms.
* Authorization checks.
* Rate limits.
* Access restrictions.
* Integrity checks.
* Update verification.
* Security monitoring controls.

============================================================
18. ACCOUNT ABUSE
=================

[MANDATORY]

Users must not create or operate accounts for abusive purposes.

This includes:

* Mass account creation intended to bypass restrictions.
* Fake accounts used for fraud.
* Accounts created to evade enforcement.
* Credential sharing where prohibited.
* Account trading where prohibited.
* Deliberate use of compromised accounts.

============================================================
19. ENFORCEMENT
===============

Where legally and contractually permitted, Weird Stuff may take
appropriate action when this Policy is violated.

Possible actions may include:

* Warning.
* Restriction.
* Temporary suspension.
* Removal of specific functionality.
* Account suspension.
* Account termination.
* Removal of prohibited content.
* Blocking abusive activity.
* Referral to another process or authority where appropriate.

[MANDATORY]

Enforcement should be proportionate to the circumstances and
consistent with applicable law and contractual obligations.

============================================================
20. IMMEDIATE SECURITY MEASURES
===============================

Where there is a credible and significant threat to users,
infrastructure, or service security:

[MANDATORY]

Weird Stuff may take immediate technical measures reasonably
necessary to contain the threat, including temporary access
restrictions or service isolation, subject to applicable law.

Such measures do not automatically determine whether the User has
violated this Policy.

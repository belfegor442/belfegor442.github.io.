1. PURPOSE
   ============================================================

This Intellectual Property Policy establishes the general rules
governing intellectual property used, created, owned, licensed,
distributed, or otherwise handled by Weird Stuff.

This Policy applies to software, source code, compiled software,
designs, graphics, branding, documentation, text, audiovisual
materials, digital assets, websites, product names, repositories,
and other intellectual property relevant to Weird Stuff.

============================================================
2. SCOPE
========

This Policy applies to:

* Weird Stuff products.
* Internal projects.
* Public releases.
* Source code repositories.
* Designs and visual assets.
* Documentation.
* Websites.
* Branding.
* Custom applications.
* Third-party software and assets.
* User-submitted materials where applicable.

[MANDATORY]

Any person acting on behalf of Weird Stuff must comply with the
intellectual property requirements applicable to their work.

============================================================
3. OWNERSHIP
============

Intellectual property rights may belong to:

* Weird Stuff.
* Individual creators.
* Contractors or collaborators.
* Third-party licensors.
* Users.
* Other rights holders.

[MANDATORY]

No person may claim ownership of intellectual property solely
because they have access to it.

[MANDATORY]

Ownership must be determined according to applicable law,
agreements, licenses, and the circumstances under which the work
was created.

============================================================

Weird Stuff may own or control rights relating to:

* Software.

* Product names.
* Logos.
* Brand assets.
* User interfaces.
* Graphics.
* Documentation.
* Websites.
* Original text.
* Original audiovisual content.
* Other original works.

[PENDING]

The complete list of registered or formally protected Weird Stuff

============================================================
5. PRODUCT INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
================================

The following Weird Stuff projects may contain intellectual

* Monix

============================================================
6. SOURCE CODE
==============

[MANDATORY]

Source code belonging to Weird Stuff must not be:

* Reproduced without authorization where prohibited.
* Re-distributed without authorization where prohibited.
* Sold without authorization where prohibited.
* Publicly disclosed where it is not intended to be public.
* Incorporated into another project in violation of applicable
  rights or licenses.

[MANDATORY]

Open-source repositories must be treated according to the license
attached to the relevant repository or component.

============================================================
7. SOFTWARE LICENSES
====================

Software made available by Weird Stuff may be distributed under
specific licenses.

[MANDATORY]

Users must comply with the license applicable to the software
they use.

[PENDING]

Each public Weird Stuff product must identify its applicable
software license before publication.

Possible licensing models may include:

* Proprietary.
* Open source.
* Free software.
* Custom license.
* Third-party licensed software.

The actual model must be determined individually for each product.

============================================================
8. OPEN SOURCE
==============

Weird Stuff may use, modify, distribute, or contribute to
open-source software.

[MANDATORY]

All applicable open-source license requirements must be respected.

This may include:

* Attribution.
* Copyright notices.
* License notices.
* Redistribution requirements.
* Source-code availability requirements where applicable.
* Modification notices where required.
* Other license conditions.

[MANDATORY]

A developer must not intentionally remove an applicable open-source
license requirement.

[RECOMMENDED]

Projects should maintain a record of relevant open-source
dependencies and their licenses.

============================================================
9. THIRD-PARTY ASSETS
=====================

Third-party assets may include:

* Fonts.
* Images.
* Icons.
* Sounds.
* Music.
* Libraries.
* Frameworks.
* Models.
* Code.
* Documentation.
* Templates.
* Other digital resources.

[MANDATORY]

Third-party assets must only be used where Weird Stuff has a
sufficient legal right or license to use them.

[MANDATORY]

The source and applicable license of important third-party assets
should be known before distribution.

============================================================
10. COPYRIGHT
=============

Copyright may apply to original works used or created by Weird
Stuff, subject to applicable law.

[MANDATORY]

Copyright notices, attribution statements, and license
requirements must be preserved when required.

[MANDATORY]

A person must not falsely claim authorship of work created by
another party.

[MANDATORY]

Original works created by third parties remain subject to the
rights of their creators unless rights have been validly
transferred, licensed, or otherwise obtained.

============================================================
11. TRADEMARKS AND BRANDING
===========================

Weird Stuff may use names, logos, visual identities, product
names, and other branding elements.

[MANDATORY]

Weird Stuff branding must not knowingly be used in a manner that
falsely suggests:

* Official endorsement.
* Official representation.
* Ownership where none exists.
* Partnership where none exists.
* Sponsorship where none exists.

[MANDATORY]

Only authorized people may approve official modifications to
company branding.

============================================================

======================

Where a person creates code, graphics, documentation, or other
material for Weird Stuff:

[MANDATORY]

The ownership and usage rights must be determined according to
applicable agreements and law.

[MANDATORY]

No person should assume that participation in a project
automatically transfers every legal right to Weird Stuff.

[PENDING]

Standard agreements governing ownership and assignment of
intellectual property have not been defined in this document.

Where external collaborators create material for Weird Stuff:

[MANDATORY]

[MANDATORY]

Standard contractor and collaborator intellectual property

15. USER CONTENT
================

Users may provide content to Weird Stuff services.

[MANDATORY]

Users must not submit material they do not have the right to use
where such submission would violate applicable law or rights.

[MANDATORY]

Weird Stuff must not claim ownership of User content solely
because the User submitted it unless an applicable agreement
expressly provides otherwise.

[PENDING]

The exact license, if any, granted by Users to Weird Stuff must
be established separately for each service that accepts User
content.

16. LICENSE GRANTS
==================

When Weird Stuff grants a User a license to software or other
intellectual property:

[MANDATORY]

The scope of the license must be determined by the applicable
license terms.

Possible license characteristics may include:

* Personal use.
* Commercial use.
* Modification.
* Distribution.
* Sublicensing.
* Term.
* Territory.
* Platform restrictions.

[PENDING]

Product-specific license terms.

============================================================
17. UNAUTHORIZED COPYING
========================

[MANDATORY]

Users and members must not knowingly reproduce or distribute
protected Weird Stuff material beyond the permissions granted by
the applicable license or law.

This may include:

* Source code.
* Proprietary binaries.
* Restricted assets.
* Internal documentation.
* Protected graphics.
* Proprietary product material.

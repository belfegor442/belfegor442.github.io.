[MANDATORY]

4. PRINCIPLES OF PROCESSING
===========================

[MANDATORY]

Weird Stuff will seek to apply the following principles to
personal data processing:

* Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency.
* Purpose limitation.
* Data minimization.
* Accuracy.
* Storage limitation.
* Integrity and confidentiality.
* Accountability.

[MANDATORY]

Personal data must not knowingly be collected or processed in a
manner incompatible with the stated or legally permitted purpose.

[MANDATORY]

Only data reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose should be
processed.

These principles are consistent with the core requirements of the
GDPR.

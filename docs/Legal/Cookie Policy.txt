1. PURPOSE
   ============================================================

This Cookie Policy explains how Weird Stuff ("Weird Stuff",
"we", "us", or "our") may use cookies and similar technologies
when Users access applicable websites, applications, or online
services.

This Policy explains the purposes of these technologies, the
categories they may belong to, and how Users may manage them.

This Policy should be read together with the Weird Stuff Privacy
Policy and Terms and Conditions.

============================================================
2. WHAT ARE COOKIES
===================

Cookies are small files or similar technologies that may be
stored on a User's device when visiting a website or using a
service.

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to:

* Keep a service functioning.
* Remember preferences.
* Maintain sessions.
* Measure usage.
* Improve services.
* Provide security features.
* Perform analytics.
* Provide other functionality.

Not every Weird Stuff service necessarily uses cookies.
